What you just heard

Four things that a purchased rehearsal track cannot do.

  • It's homemade and authentic to our choir. It's helpful to rehearse with the same voice the choir will blend with on Sunday morning.
  • The actual accompaniment we will have on Sunday. My piano, our key, our tempo, our arrangement.
  • The piano-only track offer a chance to sing the part alone. Or to just enjoy instrumental praise.
  • It is new every week. Recorded, edited weekly, dialed in for whatever we are singing that Sunday.

How a working choir actually uses it

Choir members are busy. They are often parents and hold full-time jobs. This is built to make practice as easy as pushing a button.

Don't read music? No problem! Get real rehearsal with accompaniment instead of picking out notes at a piano or trying to match the key of an artist video.

Either way, there is a "play all" button for each section that runs every song for that Sunday, in service order. Press it leaving work and you have rehearsed the whole morning by the time you pull into the driveway.

Not every song is in four parts. Unison songs and melody-driven contemporary songs are posted the way we sing them. The button gives each section what it actually sings.
